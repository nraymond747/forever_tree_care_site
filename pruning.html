Professional Pruning

Proper pruning enhances the beauty of almost any landscape tree and shrub, while improper pruning can ruin or greatly reduce its landscape potential. In most cases, it's better not to prune than to do it incorrectly.

In nature, plants go years with little or no pruning, but man can ruin what nature has created. Using improper pruning methods, healthy plants are often weakened or deformed. In the long run, a plant growing naturally assumes the shape that lets it make the best use of light in a given location and climate.

While native trees and shrubs don't need pruning, Forever Tree Care can help maintain and prolong your plants with professional pruning that enhances your landscape rather than fighting against it.
