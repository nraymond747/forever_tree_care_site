Our Story

A Tree Care Company Built On Trust

Whether a tree is hazardous, too close to your home, or you're just after a little more light in the yard, Forever Tree Care provides expert tree removal and stump grinding services across Western Massachusetts, Southern Vermont, and the surrounding areas.

We specialize in removing trees from delicate or inaccessible areas of your property, and we run the most modern, advanced stump grinding machines available to clear out any unsightly or hazardous stumps for good.

Decayed, dying, or growing too close to your home — these are the trees most likely to fail, and the ones we're called in to deal with first. Call us today to schedule a consultation.
